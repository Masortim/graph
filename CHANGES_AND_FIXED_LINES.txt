1. CRITICAL FIX IN v2.01: NODE DESIGN RESTORATION (REMOVAL OF CLOSED STROKE CONTOUR)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Bug:
  In v2.00, an unwanted stroke outline (`ctx.stroke()` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`)
  was inadvertently applied around every node circle, creating a closed border / contour.
* Fix:
  - Removed all hard stroke / border contours around standard nodes.
  - Restored original volumetric 3D spherical rendering with radial gradient:
      `fillGrad.addColorStop(0, '#ffffff');`
      `fillGrad.addColorStop(0.35, node.color || '#38bdf8');`
      `fillGrad.addColorStop(1, shadeColor(node.color || '#38bdf8', -30));`
  - Restored smooth radial halo glow on selection, hover, and pinned states.
  - Node design is now 100% identical to the original visual appearance without borders.
